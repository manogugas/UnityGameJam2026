# Laurus Racing

**Win the laurel. Survive the crowd.**

Laurus Racing is a first-person racing game set inside a Roman colosseum. You build your racer in the workshop beneath the stands, roll out through the gate, and race three laps around a figure-eight track while 7,000 spectators roar for blood. The two straights cross in the middle of the arena, so every lap risks a T-bone at full speed.

Win races to climb the championship ladder, earn prize money and spend it on the car between events. Beat the champion and the arena fights open up: last car running wins.

### Features
- **Pick your car:** front-, all- or rear-wheel drive, each with its own handling. Choose a two-digit race number that's painted on your car.
- **The emperor's mark:** before every event the emperor marks one number. Everyone hunts the marked car, and wrecking it pays triple.
- **Race with a bite:** three laps with checkpoints, boost pads, rivals who ram when they get the chance, and an optional bonnet-mounted machine gun.
- **Workshop upgrades:** a robotic arm hands you a tablet through the car window. Repair the hull, and upgrade armour, engine, tyres, ram plate, boost tank, machine gun and a traction-control switch.
- **Damage you can see:** dented panels, cracked windscreens, bumpers and mirrors that fly off, a bonnet that flaps loose.
- **Procedural sound:** the engine, tyres and crowd are synthesised live in the game.

## Controls

**Keyboard and mouse (recommended)**

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Accelerate / brake & reverse | W / S (or ↑ / ↓) |
| Steer | A / D (or ← / →) |
| Handbrake | Space |
| Boost (once fitted) | Left Shift |
| Machine gun (once fitted) | Left mouse button / Left Ctrl |
| Look left / right / back | Q / E / Alt |
| Flip the car upright | R |
| Driver's seat / 3rd-person camera | C or F4 |
| Assists on/off (after the traction-control upgrade) | B |
| Pause menu & settings | Esc |
| Debug overlay | F3 |

The workshop tablet is used with the mouse.

**Gamepad (driving only):** left stick to steer, right trigger to accelerate, left trigger to brake and reverse, B / ○ for the handbrake, bumpers to look left and right, Y / △ to look back, Select to flip upright. The boost, gun, camera and menu still need the keyboard.

**Tip:** the boost and the machine gun are upgrades. Buy them in the workshop before you can use them.
